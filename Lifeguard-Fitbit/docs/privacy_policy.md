# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** September 27, 2025

LifeGuard is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy outlines how we collect, use, and safeguard your personal data.

## Information We Collect
- Health metrics (e.g., heart rate, sleep duration, steps)
- Profile data (age, gender, weight, height)
- Device activity and location (if permission is granted)

## How We Use Your Data
- To provide personalized health insights
- To track progress toward fitness goals
- To improve app performance and user experience

## Data Sharing
We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties. Data may be shared with Fitbit or Android Health only with your consent.

## Security
We use secure protocols and encryption to protect your data. Sensitive operations (e.g., token exchange) are handled via backend services.

## Your Rights
You may request deletion of your data or revoke permissions at any time through the app settings.
